Chapter One

Like a Dream I Never Knew

The noise of the world faded into silence, the very second your eyes met mine. It felt as though reality had gently paused, giving way to a daydream I never knew existed— an unspoken warmth that swept away everything before it.
The vision that changed everything.
There you stood, radiant and effortless, outshining every definition of beauty I’d ever known. Not merely in the grace of your smile, nor the quiet elegance of your presence, but in the way your light made the whole world look softer, leaving me completely captivated.
Our beginning, captured in a frame.
And then, we stood together side by side, freezing time in that very first picture of us. That was the precise instant my heart surrendered— quietly, wholly, and without a single doubt. From that moment forward, I was no longer just me; I was completely, effortlessly yours.
